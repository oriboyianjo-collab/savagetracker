“The fourth quarter showed momentum building as the sales environment improved and we realized the full program targeted run rate of $25 million of annualized savings under Project Apollo,” said Dan Fachner, President and CEO of J&J Snack Foods. “Gross margin expanded 200 basis points to 33.7% and we delivered Adjusted EBITDA of $53.3 million and Adjusted earnings per diluted share of $1.39, even as distribution costs increased by 21.6% amid a significant increase in freight and fuel costs. Net sales declined 1.0%. Food Service sales declined 7.4%, driven primarily by anticipated bakery reductions that outweighed growth in core churro and pretzel products, which more than offset a 17.1% increase in Retail sales and a 6.1% increase in Frozen Beverage sales.

“We expect to return to top-line growth in fiscal 2027 through core volume growth, innovation and pricing. Gross margin expanded by 210 basis points in fiscal 2026, supported by delivery of our Project Apollo initiatives and mix improvements. The next phase of Project Apollo is expected to generate an incremental $5 million in annualized run rate savings by focusing on further manufacturing efficiencies and distribution cost initiatives. With a stronger core portfolio, growing innovation pipeline, more efficient cost structure and a strong balance sheet, we are well positioned entering fiscal 2027.”

Fourth Quarter Results

Net sales decreased 1.0% from the prior year quarter to $406.0 million.

●

Food Service segment net sales decreased 7.4%

●

Retail Supermarket segment net sales increased 17.1%

●

Frozen Beverage segment net sales increased 6.1%

Gross profit increased from $130.2 million in the prior year quarter to $136.9 million, while gross margin improved from 31.7% to 33.7%. The improvement in gross margin was supported by plant consolidation initiatives from Project Apollo, mix improvements, and raw material deflation, partially offset by higher promotional investment.

Total operating expenses of $92.0 million represented 22.7% of sales for the quarter, compared to 29.0% in the prior year quarter. Operating expenses in the quarter included a net non-recurring gain of approximately $11.9 million, largely driven by a $16 million gain from final insurance proceeds related to the Holly Ridge plant fire. Prior year quarter operating expenses included non-recurring operating expenses of approximately $24.8 million, driven primarily by $24.1 million of plant closure expenses.

●

Selling and Marketing expenses increased 1.6% to $33.1 million or 8.2% of sales, up from 7.9% in the prior year quarter.

●

Distribution expenses increased 21.6% to $51.3 million or 12.6% of sales, up from 10.3% in the prior year quarter. Distribution expenses increased primarily due to significantly higher freight and fuel rates.

●

Administrative expenses increased 15.3% to $22.0 million or 5.4% of sales, up from 4.7% in the prior year quarter. The increase primarily was due to $2.7 million of non-recurring legal expenses.

Operating income was $44.9 million, compared to $11.5 million in the prior year quarter, while adjusted operating income was $34.5 million, compared to $37.7 million in the prior year quarter. Earnings per diluted share were $1.80, compared to $0.58 in the prior year quarter, while adjusted earnings per diluted share were $1.39, compared to $1.58 in the prior year quarter. The effective tax rate was 25.7%, compared to 4.8% in the prior year quarter. The lower effective tax rate in the prior year quarter primarily reflected a change in estimate on our blended state tax rate, and the corresponding impact on the valuation of our net deferred tax liabilities.

Food Service Segment

●

Net sales of $240.2 million, a year-over-year decrease of $19.1 million or 7.4%.

●

Anticipated reductions in our bakery business represented approximately half of the year-over-year decrease in net sales.

●

Growth in churros and pretzels was more than offset by softness in cookies and handhelds.

●

Operating income decreased $2.3 million to $19.8 million as higher distribution costs more than offset the increase in gross profit.

Retail Supermarket Segment

●

Net sales of $60.2 million, a year-over-year increase of $8.8 million or 17.1%.

●

Growth was driven by an increase in frozen novelties and churros.

●

Operating income decreased $3.9 million to a loss of $1.5 million, driven by an increase in promotional investment, higher distribution costs, and favorable insurance recoveries in the prior year.

Frozen Beverages Segment

●

Net sales of $105.7 million, a year-over-year increase of $6.1 million or 6.1%.

●

Beverage sales were up 19%, partially offset by lower machine and service sales.

●

Operating income increased $2.7 million to $21.4 million.

Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and Capital Allocation

The Company ended the fourth quarter with cash of $90.3 million and no long-term debt. During the fourth quarter, the Company generated operating cash flow of $69.7 million and invested $17.6 million in capital expenditures. For fiscal 2026, operating cash flow was $170.2 million and capital expenditures were $70.9 million. During fiscal 2026, 854,208 shares of common stock were repurchased for $74.7 million.

About J & J Snack Foods Corp.

J & J Snack Foods Corp. (Nasdaq: JJSF) is a leader and innovator in the snack food and frozen beverage industry. For over fifty years, the company has specialized in delicious snack and beverage brands for the foodservice and retail segments, serving up fun across the U.S. market. J & J Snack Foods’ core brands include SUPERPRETZEL, the #1 soft pretzel brand, ICEE and SLUSH PUPPIE frozen beverages, and Dippin’ Dots, the original beaded ice cream. The company’s broad brand portfolio also includes LUIGI’S Real Italian Ice, MINUTE MAID* frozen ices, WHOLE FRUIT frozen fruit bars, DOGSTERS ice cream style treats for dogs, ¡Hola! Churros, THE FUNNEL CAKE FACTORY funnel cakes and fries, and bakery brands including MARY B’S, DADDY RAY’S, COUNTRY HOME BAKERS, and HILL & VALLEY. For more information, please visit http://www.jjsnack.com. *MINUTE MAID is a registered trademark of The Coca-Cola Company.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Adjusted Operating Income consists of operating income adjusted to exclude: impairment charges, restructuring costs, merger and acquisition costs, acquisition related amortization expenses and inventory adjustments, strategic business transformation costs, integration costs, non-recurring legal expenses, gain on insurance proceeds received for damage to property, plant and equipment, and plant closure expenses. Adjusted Earnings per Diluted Share consists of net earnings adjusted to exclude: impairment charges, restructuring costs, merger and acquisition costs, acquisition related amortization expenses and inventory adjustment, strategic business transformation costs, integration costs, non-recurring legal expenses, gain on insurance proceeds received for damage to property, plant and equipment, and plant closure expenses. For purposes of comparability, the income tax effect of pre-tax adjustments is determined using statutory tax rates.
