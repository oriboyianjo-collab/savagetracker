LOWE’S REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 SALES AND EARNINGS RESULTS
— Diluted EPS of $2.98; Adjusted Diluted EPS1 of $3.10 —
— Total Sales Increased 9.3% —
— Comparable Sales Increased 1.8% —
— Updates Full Year 2026 Outlook —

MOORESVILLE, N.C., November 18, 2026 – Lowe’s Companies, Inc. (NYSE: LOW) today reported net earnings of $1.7 billion and diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $2.98 for the quarter ended October 30, 2026, compared to diluted EPS of $2.88 in the third quarter of 2025. During the third quarter ended October 30, 2026, the company recognized $96 million in pre-tax expenses associated with acquisition-related intangible asset amortization for Foundation Building Materials (FBM) and Artisan Design Group (ADG). Excluding these expenses, third quarter 2026 adjusted diluted EPS1 increased 1.3% to $3.10 compared to the prior-year adjusted diluted EPS. The company also recognized a positive pre-tax impact of approximately $250 million associated with IEEPA tariff refunds. Both diluted EPS and adjusted diluted EPS1 include a $0.34 positive impact from IEEPA tariff refunds.

Total sales for the quarter were $22.8 billion,compared to $20.8 billion in the prior-year quarter. Comparable sales for the quarter increased 1.8%, driven by a 17.4% increase in online sales as well as solid performance in Pro and home services sales.

“We just delivered our sixth consecutive quarter of positive comp sales driven by continued growth across Pro, Online and Home Services. These results reflect the resiliency of our Total Home strategy,” said Marvin R. Ellison, Lowe’s chairman, president and CEO. “We remain confident in our agile business model and look forward to sharing more about our growth opportunities in December at our Analyst and Investor Conference. I would also like to thank our frontline associates for their dedication to serve our customers by solving problems and fulfilling dreams for their homes.”

As of October 30, 2026, Lowe’s operated 1,762 stores, representing 197.0 million square feet of retail selling space.

Capital Allocation
The company remains committed to generating sustainable shareholder value through a disciplined focus on its capital allocation program. During the quarter, the company paid $701 million in dividends and maintained its status as a Dividend Aristocrat and Dividend King.

Lowe’s Companies, Inc.

Lowe's Companies, Inc. (NYSE: LOW) is a FORTUNE® 100 home improvement company with total fiscal year 2025 sales of more than $86 billion. Lowe's employs approximately 300,000 associates and operates over 1,750 home improvement stores, 540 branches and 120 distribution centers. Lowe’s is a core value S&P 500 equity stock, a dividend aristocrat and dividend king. Based in Mooresville, N.C., Lowe's supports the communities it serves through programs focused on creating safe, affordable housing, improving community spaces, helping to develop the next generation of skilled trade experts and providing disaster relief to communities in need. For more information, visit Lowes.com.

•In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company recognized pre-tax expenses of $96 million consisting of intangible asset amortization related to the acquisitions of Artisan Design Group and Foundation Building Materials (Acquisitions of businesses).

Fiscal 2025 Impacts

•In the third quarter of fiscal 2025, the Company recognized pre-tax expenses of $129 million consisting of transaction costs and intangible asset amortization related to the acquisitions of Artisan Design Group and Foundation Building Materials (Acquisitions of businesses).
