THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. REPORTS Q3 FY27 RESULTS; COMP SALES GROWTH OF 4%, PRETAX PROFIT MARGIN, AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE ALL ABOVE PLAN

Adjusted results throughout this press release exclude a benefit from International Emergency Economic Powers Act (“IEEPA”) tariff refunds that are partially offset by related incremental compensation expense accruals (see below)

•Q3 consolidated comparable sales increased 4%, which was above the Company’s plan
•Q3 Marmaxx comparable sales improved to a 2% increase on top of a 6% increase in the prior year and the Company saw continued strength at its HomeGoods, TJX Canada, and TJX International divisions
•Q3 pretax profit margin of 13.0%, up 0.3 percentage points versus last year and above the Company’s plan
•Q3 adjusted pretax profit margin of 12.5%, down 0.2 percentage points versus last year and above the Company’s plan
•Q3 diluted earnings per share of $1.39, up 9% versus last year and above the Company’s plan
•Q3 adjusted diluted earnings per share of $1.34, up 5% versus last year and above the Company’s plan
•Returned $1.5 billion to shareholders in Q3 through share repurchases and dividends

Framingham, MA – The TJX Companies, Inc. (NYSE: TJX), the leading off-price apparel and home fashions retailer in the U.S. and worldwide, today announced sales and operating results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026. Net sales for the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 were $16.0 billion, an increase of 6% versus the third quarter of Fiscal 2026. Third quarter Fiscal 2027 consolidated comparable sales increased 4%. Net income for the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $1.5 billion. Third quarter Fiscal 2027 diluted earnings per share were $1.39, up 9% versus $1.28 in the third quarter of Fiscal 2026. Excluding a $.05 net benefit from tariff refunds, third quarter Fiscal 2027 adjusted diluted earnings per share were $1.34, up 5% versus the prior year.

For the first nine months of Fiscal 2027, net sales were $45.5 billion, an increase of 7% versus the first nine months of Fiscal 2026. Consolidated comparable sales for the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 increased 4%. Net income for the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 was $4.4 billion. For the first nine months of Fiscal 2027, diluted earnings per share were $3.94, up 19% versus $3.30 in the first nine months of Fiscal 2026. Excluding a $.19 net benefit from tariff refunds, adjusted diluted earnings per share for the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 were $3.75, up 14% versus the prior year.




CEO and President Comments

Ernie Herrman, Chief Executive Officer and President of The TJX Companies, Inc., stated, “I am very pleased with our consolidated third quarter results. Overall sales, profitability, and earnings per share all exceeded our expectations. Consolidated comparable sales grew 4%, driven by continued strength at HomeGoods, TJX Canada, and TJX International, and we saw improved sales at Marmaxx. Importantly, we expect further improvement at Marmaxx in the fourth quarter. We believe our above-plan results in the third quarter highlight the benefit of our global, diversified business. Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, while we believe the unseasonably warm weather in the U.S. and Canada has been holding back consumer demand in some categories, we are expecting sales momentum to accelerate throughout the holiday season. We are confident in our plans for the holiday selling season and availability of quality merchandise continues to be outstanding. All of our retail banners are very strongly positioned as gifting destinations and we are confident that consumers will be very pleased with our holiday gift giving assortment. Further, we will be flowing fresh merchandise and exciting gifts to our stores and online multiple times a week throughout the season. Longer term, we remain energized by the opportunities we see to further drive sales, grow our retail footprint, and keep capturing market share around the globe.”

Margins

For the third quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company’s pretax profit margin was 13.0%, up 0.3 percentage points versus last year’s 12.7%. Excluding a 0.5 percentage point net benefit from tariff refunds, adjusted pretax profit margin was 12.5%, down 0.2 percentage points versus the prior year.

Gross profit margin for the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 was 32.9%, up 0.3 percentage points versus last year’s 32.6%. Excluding a 0.8 percentage point net benefit from tariff refunds, adjusted gross profit margin was 32.1%, down 0.5 percentage points versus the prior year. This was driven by higher freight and fuel costs.

SG&A costs as a percent of sales for the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 were 20.1%, flat versus last year. Excluding a 0.3 percentage point negative impact from tariff refund related incremental compensation expense accruals, adjusted SG&A costs as a percent of sales were 19.8%, 0.3 percentage points favorable versus the prior year. This was driven by lower incentive compensation accruals and a benefit from lapping a contribution made to the TJX Foundation last year.

Net interest income had a neutral impact to third quarter Fiscal 2027 pretax profit margin versus the prior year.

Inventory

Total inventories as of October 31, 2026 were $9.8 billion, compared to $9.4 billion at the end of the third quarter of Fiscal 2026. Consolidated inventories on a per-store basis as of October 31, 2026, including distribution centers, but excluding inventory in transit and the Company’s e-commerce sites, were flat on both a reported and constant currency basis, versus last year. The Company is well-positioned to take advantage of the outstanding availability it is seeing in the marketplace and deliver exciting merchandise and gifts to its stores and online this holiday season. Inventory on a constant currency basis reflects inventory adjusted for the impact of foreign currency, if any, as described below.

Cash and Shareholder Distributions

For the third quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company generated $1.7 billion of operating cash flow and ended the quarter with $4.6 billion of cash. During the third quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company paid down its $1.0 billion 2.25% senior note that matured on September 15, 2026.

During the third quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company returned a total of $1.5 billion to shareholders. The Company repurchased 7.3 million shares of TJX stock for a total of $1.0 billion and paid $529 million in shareholder dividends.

During the first nine months of Fiscal 2027, the Company returned a total of $3.9 billion to shareholders. The Company repurchased 16.2 million shares of TJX stock for a total of $2.4 billion and paid $1.5 billion in shareholder dividends.

The Company now expects to repurchase approximately $3.0 billion of TJX stock during Fiscal 2027. The Company may adjust the amount purchased under this plan up or down depending on various factors. The Company remains committed to returning cash to its shareholders while continuing to invest in the business to support the near- and long-term growth of TJX.

IEEPA Tariff Refunds and Related Expense Accruals

During the third quarter and first nine months of Fiscal 2027, the Company received aggregate refunds of $132 million and $463 million, respectively, for a portion of the IEEPA tariffs it previously paid. As a result, these tariff refunds benefited the Company’s cost of sales during the respective periods. Due to these tariff refunds, the Company accrued incremental expenses during the third quarter and first nine months of Fiscal 2027 of $55 million and $167 million, respectively, for year-end incentive compensation and non-executive discretionary bonuses for eligible Associates globally, which impact both cost of sales and SG&A costs during the respective periods. The net benefit of tariff refunds was $77 million and $296 million, respectively, for the Company’s pretax profit in the third quarter and first nine months of Fiscal 2027.

Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2027 Outlook

In the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company is not expecting additional tariff refunds, but does expect to accrue incremental expenses for year-end incentive compensation and discretionary bonuses related to the tariff refunds received in the second and third quarters of Fiscal 2027, which would impact the Company’s fourth quarter Fiscal 2027 cost of sales and SG&A costs.

For the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2027, the Company is planning consolidated comparable sales to be up 2% to 3%. The Company expects pretax profit margin to be in the range of 11.5% to 11.6%. Excluding an expected negative impact of 0.3 percentage points from tariff refund related incremental compensation expense accruals, the Company expects adjusted pretax profit margin to be in the range of 11.8% to 11.9%. The Company expects diluted earnings per share to be in the range of $1.39 to $1.42. Excluding an expected negative impact of $.03 from tariff refund related incremental compensation expense accruals, the Company expects adjusted diluted earnings per share to be in the range of $1.42 to $1.45. The Company’s fourth quarter pretax profit margin and diluted earnings per share outlook now assumes additional fuel costs versus its prior guidance.

For the full year Fiscal 2027, the Company now expects consolidated comparable sales to be up 4%. The Company expects its full-year pretax profit margin to be 12.4%. Excluding an expected net benefit of 0.3 to 0.4 percentage points from tariff refunds, the Company continues to expect its full-year adjusted pretax profit margin to be in the range of 12.0% to 12.1%. The Company expects its full-year diluted earnings per share outlook to be in the range of $5.33 to $5.36. Excluding an expected net benefit of $.16 from tariff refunds, the Company expects its full year outlook for adjusted diluted earnings per share to be in the range of $5.17 to $5.20.

Impact of Foreign Currency

Changes in foreign currency exchange rates affect the translation of sales and earnings of the Company’s international businesses into U.S. dollars for financial reporting purposes. In addition, ordinary course, inventory-related hedging instruments are marked to market at the end of each quarter. Changes in currency exchange rates can have a material effect on the magnitude of these translations and adjustments when there is significant volatility in currency exchange rates. Given the global operations of the Company, to facilitate comparability, the Company has provided sales growth and inventory on a constant currency basis, which assumes a constant exchange rate between periods for translation based on the rate in effect for the prior period.

The movement in foreign currency exchange rates had a neutral impact on the Company’s net sales growth in the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 versus the prior year. The overall net impact of foreign currency exchange rates had a $.01 negative impact on third quarter Fiscal 2027 diluted earnings per share.

The movement in foreign currency exchange rates had a one percentage point positive impact on the Company’s net sales growth in the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 versus the prior year. The overall net impact of foreign currency exchange rates had a $.01 positive impact on the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 diluted earnings per share.

The foreign currency exchange rate impact to diluted earnings per share does not include the impact currency exchange rates have on various transactions, which the Company refers to as “transactional foreign exchange.”

Additionally, a table detailing the impact of foreign currency on TJX’s net sales and pretax profit margin, as well as those of its international businesses, can be found in the Investors section of TJX.com.

About The TJX Companies, Inc.

The TJX Companies, Inc., a Fortune 100 company, is the leading off-price retailer of apparel and home fashions in the U.S. and worldwide. Our mission is to deliver great value to customers every day. We do this by offering a rapidly changing assortment of quality, fashionable, brand name, and designer merchandise at prices generally 20% to 60% below full-price retailers’ regular prices on comparable merchandise. We operate over 5,300 stores across ten countries, including TJ Maxx, Marshalls, HomeGoods, Homesense, and Sierra in the U.S.; Winners, HomeSense, and Marshalls in Canada; TK Maxx and Homesense in Europe; and TK Maxx in Australia. We also operate e-commerce sites for TJ Maxx, Marshalls, and Sierra in the U.S. and three e-commerce sites for TK Maxx in Europe. Our value mission extends to our corporate responsibility efforts, which are focused on supporting our Associates, giving back in the communities we serve, the environment, and operating responsibly.
