Kandi Technologies Group, Inc., a British Virgin Islands company (the “Company”), furnishes under the cover of Form 6-K its financial results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026:

Nine Months 2026 Financial Focus

● Nine-month revenues of $82.1 million increased 38.5% year-over-year.
● Nine-month revenue from off-road vehicles and associated parts increased by 33.5% year-over-year to $74.0 million, compared to $55.4 million in the same period of 2025.
● Nine-month net income was $5.6 million, or $0.57 per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $6.3 million, or $0.75 per basic and diluted share, for the same period of 2025.1
● Cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash and certificates of deposit totaled $291.3 million as of September 30, 2026.

● Net revenues in the first three quarters of 2026 were $82.1 million compared to $59.3 million in the same period of 2025, representing an increase of 38.5%, mainly due to an increase in sales of off-road vehicles compared to the prior period, as well as the additional revenue contributed by Rawrr Inc. The Company completed the acquisition of Rawrr Inc. in February 2026, pursuant to the share transfer agreement dated December 5, 2025.
● Cost of goods sold in the first three quarters of 2026 was $50.4 million compared to $32.3 million in the same period of 2025, representing an increase of 56.0%. The increase was primarily due to the corresponding increase in sales.
● Gross profit in the first three quarters of 2026 was $31.7 million, compared to $26.9 million in the same period of 2025, representing an increase of 17.6%. The overall gross margin for the first three quarters was 38.6% compared to 45.5% in the same period of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to product mix, as well as the product sales of Rawrr Inc. offer relatively lower gross margin.
