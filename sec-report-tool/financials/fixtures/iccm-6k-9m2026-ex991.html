IceCure Reports Financial & Operational Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026: 83% Year-Over-Year Growth in U.S. Sales and Major Growth in U.S. Commercial Reach to 21 States

CAESAREA, Israel, November 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- IceCure Medical Ltd. (Nasdaq: ICCM) (“IceCure,” “IceCure Medical” or the “Company”), developer of minimally invasive cryoablation technology that destroys tumors by freezing as an option to surgical tumor removal, today reported its financial and operating results as of and for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Operational and Clinical Highlights

●83% Growth in U.S. Sales: U.S. sales of ProSense® and its cryoprobes doubled year over year during the first nine months of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025.
●26% Year-Over-Year Revenue Growth: Total revenues for the first nine months of 2026 increased to approximately $2.6 million, compared to $2.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025.
●Expanded U.S. Commercial Reach: The Company’s commercial presence expanded from 11 states in the third quarter of 2025 to 21 states in the third quarter of 2026.
●Continued Progress in the ChoICE Study: The Company’s post-marketing study (the “ChoICE” Study”) evaluating ProSense® cryoablation for the local treatment of low-risk breast cancer launched the first clinical sites after Institutional Review Board (“IRB”) approval at West Cancer Center & Research Institute (the “West Cancer Center”) in Germantown, TN and Adventist Health Glendale in Glendale, CA.
●International Commercial and Regulatory Progress: Received regulatory approval from China’s National Medical Products Administration to market ProSense® as a Class III medical device and signed an exclusive distribution agreement for ProSense® cryoablation systems in the Netherlands.

“We believe the first nine months of 2026 demonstrate the progress we made in translating our regulatory and clinical achievements into commercial growth, particularly in the United States. U.S. sales increased 83% year over year, while our commercial reach expanded from 11 to 21 states during the third quarter of 2026. At the same time, we are building the clinical foundation for broader adoption of ProSense® through the ChoICE Study. We believe these developments position us to continue driving physician adoption and expanding patient access to ProSense®,” said Eyal Shamir, Chief Executive Officer of IceCure Medical.

ChoICE Study Moving Forward:

Subsequent to the end of the third quarter of 2026, IceCure announced additional progress in its ChoICE Study:

●Three clinical sites joined the Company’s study, including Legacy Health in Portland, Oregon.
●The Company announced the enrollment of the first two patients in the ChoICE Study at two U.S. clinical sites: West Cancer Center in Tennessee and Adventist Health Glendale in California.
●The Company announced the successful completion of the first ProSense® cryoablation treatment in the ChoICE Study. The first patient was treated at West Cancer Center in Germantown, Tennessee, by Dr. Richard E. Fine, MD, FACS.

Additional Third Quarter of 2026 Successes:

●Expanded U.S. Commercial Team: Strengthened the U.S. sales and marketing organization by adding dedicated professionals in key territories, including New York, Southern California and the Midwest, and Florida, as well as additional clinical support personnel to support physician training and adoption of ProSense®.
●Participation in Leading Medical Events: Participated in key medical and industry events, including Aptitude Health’s Targeted Medical Education (“TME”) Take the Lead in Breast Cancer Fall Summit 2026 in New Orleans, LA and the 2026 European Society of Breast Imaging (“EUSOBI”) Congress in Maastricht, Netherlands, engaging with leading breast cancer specialists and the broader medical community.
●Strengthened Clinical Evidence: Highlighted two new peer-reviewed scientific publications supporting the role of cryoablation in breast cancer treatment de-escalation, including publications addressing early-stage breast cancer and axillary lymph node metastases.
●Professional Recognition of Cryoablation: The Society of Interventional Oncology (“SIO”) submitted a formal petition to the National Comprehensive Cancer Network (“NCCN”) to include percutaneous cryoablation as a treatment option in its breast cancer guidelines, potentially broadening the range of treatment options available to eligible patients.

Financial Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

Total revenues for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased approximately 26% to $2.6 million, compared to $2.1 million for the corresponding period in 2025.

Gross profit for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased approximately 22% to $762,000, compared to $626,000 in the corresponding period of 2025.

Research and development expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $6.5 million, compared to $5.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025, primarily reflecting increased expenses related to the ChoICE Study and other clinical and development activities.

Sales and marketing expenses were $3.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $3.0 million in the corresponding period of 2025, primarily reflecting the continued expansion of the Company’s U.S. commercial activities.

General and administrative expenses were $3.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $3.3 million in the corresponding period of 2025.

Operating loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $12.9 million, compared to $10.8 million in the corresponding period of 2025.

Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $12.9 million, compared to $10.8 million in the corresponding period of 2025.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s cash and cash equivalents totaled approximately $8.2 million, and shareholders’ equity was approximately $8.7 million.

About IceCure Medical

IceCure Medical (NASDAQ: ICCM) develops and markets advanced liquid-nitrogen-based cryoablation therapy systems for the destruction of tumors (benign and cancerous) by freezing, as a safe and effective minimally invasive option to surgical tumor removal, with the primary focus areas being breast, kidney, bone and lung cancer. The Company’s flagship ProSense® system is marketed and sold worldwide for the indications cleared and approved to date including in the U.S., Europe, and Asia.

ProSense® cryoablation became the first-and-only medical device cleared by the FDA for the local treatment of breast cancer in patients aged 70 years and older with biologically low-risk tumors ≤1.5 cm in size who are treated with adjuvant endocrine therapy, including patients who are not eligible for surgery.
